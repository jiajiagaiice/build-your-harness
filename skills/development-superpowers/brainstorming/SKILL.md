# Brainstorming

- Restate the user's outcome in concrete product terms.
- Identify constraints, unknowns, risks, and non-goals.
- Compare viable approaches briefly, then choose the smallest approach that satisfies the goal.
- Convert the chosen approach into acceptance criteria that can be validated later.
